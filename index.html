Sign-in stays with your university. You log in on your official SSO portal in your normal browser. The app never sees your password.

Built for university students.

Skip clicking through 50 separate PDF links the night before a midterm.

Bulk sync

Downloads slides, syllabus files and problem sets for every course simultaneously. Your files land in folders like MTH 201/ and PHY 201/, ready to use offline.

Any major

Reads your Blackboard portal directly, so Engineering, Business, Arts, Sciences and Medicine students all see their own registered courses.

Smart incremental sync

Skips files you already have and only picks up new uploads when a professor posts them.

Private by design

Your session and your files stay entirely on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Free and open source

No paywall and no subscription traps. Built by a student so classmates can focus on studying.

Set up in under a minute.

Three steps, and the first one only happens once.

  1. Connect your account

    Click Connect Account. Your real browser opens to your university's official Blackboard page. Sign in securely and approve any 2FA prompts.

  2. Select your courses

    The app lists all your registered semester courses. Select all or choose only the classes you need.

  3. Start extraction

    Click Start Extraction and watch the live log as your lectures are organized into your chosen folder.
